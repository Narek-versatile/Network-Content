Part 2: Domain and Hosting

1. Where can you buy a domain name?

You can buy domain names from domain registrars such as namecheap, godaddy, etc.

2. Where can you buy hosting or VPS services?

Any service provider that provides virtual private servers or cloud hosting can be used. For example DigitalOcean, Vultr, AWS, GCP, etc.

3. What information should you consider before buying a domain?

You should consider the following factors before buying a domain name: price, availability, expiration date, auto-renewal, transfer policy, domain name suggestions if needed, and etc.

4. What factors should you consider before buying hosting or a VPS?

When buying a VPS you should considr factors like uptime, latency, scalability, price, community, reviews and etc.

5. Why do companies use cloud hosting?

Companies use cloud hosting because it is scalable, reliable, and cost-effective. It allows them to scale their resources up or down as needed, ensuring high availability and performance without the need for maintaining their own physical infrastructure.

6. Why do websites need both a domain and hosting?

Domain is the address of the website and hosting is the action of making your service accessible to the users. without both you can't have a website.
